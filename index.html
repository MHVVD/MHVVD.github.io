AutoPark: Vision-Based Autonomous Parking

A simulated car drives down a parking aisle, finds a free space using only its four fisheye cameras and wheel odometry, then reverses into it on its own. Every part of the stack is built from scratch in ROS 2 and Webots: a bird's-eye view from the cameras, a CNN slot detector, a Kalman filter per slot, a Hybrid A* planner with Reeds-Shepp curves and a Stanley controller.
